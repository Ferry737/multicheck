## Deutsch
### Satzbau (sentence structure / word order)
- **Evidence**: workplace sentences, main + subordinate clauses.
- **Required ability**: Subject–Verb–Object; Verb-final in subordinate clauses; comma rules.
- **Prerequisite**: basic vocabulary.
- **Question families**: scramble→order; build with Nebensatz (weil/obwohl/wenn); correct punctuation.
- **Difficulty progression**: simple 5-word → subordinate clause → two clauses.
- **Target timing**: 20s (easy) → 35s (subordinate).
- **Common mistakes**: verb position in Nebensatz; missing comma.
- **Teaching**: show correct pattern, then guided build, then independent.

### Textverständnis (reading comprehension)
- **Evidence**: short notices, opening hours, shipping notes, instructions.
- **Required ability**: locate explicit info; infer simple intent.
- **Question families**: multiple-choice on stated facts; "wann/wo/warum".
- **Difficulty**: 1–3 short texts → longer multi-fact.
- **Target timing**: 30s.
- **Common mistakes**: surface reading; confusing similar options.
- **Teaching**: highlight the exact sentence; verify against text.

## Mathematik
### Textaufgaben (word problems)
- **Concepts**: percentages, money/discount+voucher, quantities, units, time, everyday calc, multi-step.
- **Required ability**: translate text→operation; compute; sanity-check.
- **Prerequisite**: arithmetic, fractions.
- **Question families**: percent of N; discount; discount+voucher (multi-step); word sum; fraction add.
- **Difficulty**: small base → large base → multi-step with unit conversion.
- **Target timing**: 25–35s.
- **Common mistakes**: wrong operation; discount/voucher order; comma error.
- **Teaching**: step-by-step worked example; then guided; then independent.

### Kopfrechnen & Umwandeln (mental arithmetic & conversion)
- **Concepts**: +−× mental; unit conversion (kg↔g, h↔min, m↔cm, t↔kg, km↔m).
- **Required ability**: fluent arithmetic; conversion factors.
- **Question families**: pure mental; convert; convert-then-apply (two-step).
- **Difficulty**: single op → conversion → two-step conversion+op.
- **Target timing**: 12–18s.
- **Common mistakes**: factor forgotten; step order.
- **Teaching**: memorize factors; drill; speed mode.

## Logik
### Prozesslogik (process logic)
- **Concepts**: natural ordering; conditional constraints.
- **Required ability**: sequence reasoning; honor conditions.
- **Question families**: order steps; order WITH constraint (e.g. Freigabe nach Prüfung).
- **Difficulty**: 4 steps → 5 steps with condition.
- **Target timing**: 22–30s.
- **Common mistakes**: ignoring condition; wrong first/last step.
- **Teaching**: identify dependency; verify constraint.

### Wortgruppen (odd-one-out / categories)
- **Concepts**: category detection; semantic exclusion.
- **Required ability**: find the shared property; exclude the outlier.
- **Question families**: pick the word NOT in the group (prompt describes the ACTUAL group generated).
- **Difficulty**: obvious category → subtle; 3 vs 5 options.
- **Target timing**: 18s.
- **Common mistakes**: misreading the category; prompt/group mismatch (fixed: prompt now reflects generated group).
- **Teaching**: name the category; confirm outlier.

## Konzentration
### Bilder zählen (count symbols in grid)
- **Concepts**: systematic visual scanning; counting.
- **Required ability**: row-by-row count; avoid double/miss.
- **Question families**: count a target symbol in an n×n SVG grid.
- **Difficulty**: 4×4 → 6×6; more near-miss distractors at higher difficulty.
- **Target timing**: 25s.
- **Common mistakes**: skipping; double-counting.
- **Teaching**: trace a path; recount.

### Symbole entdecken (find target symbol count)
- **Concepts**: visual search; symbol discrimination.
- **Same as Bilder zählen** with a specified symbol.
- **Difficulty**: 4×4 → 6×6.
- **Target timing**: 22s.

## Merkfähigkeit
### Schilder erinnern (memory of signs)
- **Concepts**: short-term visual memory; recognition (Ja/Nein).
- **Required ability**: encode stimulus; recognize presence/absence after hiding.
- **Question families**: show n signs for memorizeMs; ask "War das Schild X dabei?" → Ja (present) / Nein (absent), balanced ~50/50.
- **Difficulty**: 3 → 6 signs; strict memorizeMs in exam mode.
- **Target timing**: 15s after hide.
- **Common mistakes**: forgetting after attention shift.
- **Teaching**: active encoding strategy ("say it once"); strict timing in exam.

## Praktisches Grundwissen
### Sortierverfahren (sorting)
- **Concepts**: ascending order; comparison.
- **Question families**: sort 4 numbers ascending.
- **Difficulty**: 2-digit → mixed.
- **Target timing**: 18s.

### Praktisches Alltagswissen (practical everyday safety)
- **Concepts**: safety priority; emergency procedure; IT security.
- **Question families**: correct FIRST action (fire, collapse, USB stick, alarm).
- **Difficulty**: single correct → multi-step ordered.
- **Target timing**: 16–22s.
- **Common mistakes**: wrong priority; ignoring safety/meldepflicht.
- **Teaching**: state the rule; drill priority.

## Textschreiben (separate activity, not drilled with computed questions)
- **Concepts**: task completion, structure, clarity, grammar, word order, vocabulary, sentence quality.
- **Evaluated by rubric** (Phase 19), not computed answer key.
- **AI role**: personalized feedback on the rubric dimensions (when available).
